Health data

FitnessTracker reads and writes the health data types you grant it access to through Android Health Connect. This data is sent to FitnessTracker's own backend to power the features you use in the app, such as your weight dashboard, workout history and nutrition tracking. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running the app

You can review or revoke this access at any time from Health Connect's own settings, or by disconnecting Health Connect from within FitnessTracker, which also deletes every sample it contributed
